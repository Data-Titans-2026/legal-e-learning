CONSUMER RIGHTS — Key Concepts
================================
Consumer protection laws give buyers rights against deceptive practices, defective goods, and abusive debt collection.

Refunds & Returns
-----------------
Retailers must honour posted return policies. Implied warranty of merchantability means goods must work as expected. Lemon laws protect buyers of repeatedly defective vehicles.

FDCPA (Debt Collection)
------------------------
Collectors cannot harass, use abusive language, call before 8am/after 9pm, contact your workplace if prohibited, or make false statements. You can demand written validation and request they stop contacting you.

FCRA (Credit Reports)
---------------------
You're entitled to a free annual credit report from each of the three major bureaus. Dispute inaccuracies in writing. Bureaus have 30 days to investigate. Negative items drop off after 7 years.

FTC Cooling-Off Rule
--------------------
You have 3 business days to cancel purchases over $25 made at home solicitations or away from a business's permanent location.

Deceptive Advertising
---------------------
The FTC prohibits false or misleading advertising. "Bait and switch" is illegal. Price gouging during emergencies violates most state laws.
