PROPERTY DISPUTES — Key Concepts
================================

Landlord-Tenant Disputes
-------------------------
Common issues: security deposit disputes, habitability problems, evictions, lease violations.
- Landlords must provide habitable premises (heat, water, structural safety).
- Security deposits must typically be returned within 14–30 days of move-out with itemised deductions.
- Evictions require proper legal notice (often 30–60 days) and court proceedings.

Property Boundaries & Easements
---------------------------------
Boundary disputes often require a licensed surveyor. Easements grant others the right to use part of your property (utility easements, right-of-way). Adverse possession can transfer title after continuous, open use for a statutory period (often 10–20 years).

Mortgages & Foreclosure
------------------------
Lenders can foreclose if borrowers default. Judicial foreclosure requires court proceedings; non-judicial (power of sale) does not. Borrowers often have a redemption period. Loss mitigation options: loan modification, forbearance, short sale, deed-in-lieu.

Co-ownership Disputes
---------------------
Tenancy in common: each owner has undivided interest, heirs can inherit. Joint tenancy: right of survivorship — deceased owner's share passes to co-owners. Disputes can lead to partition actions (court-ordered sale or division).

HOA Disputes
------------
HOAs can impose fines, place liens, and enforce CC&Rs. Homeowners can challenge fines by attending board meetings, reviewing bylaws, or filing complaints with state regulators.
