FAMILY LAW — Key Concepts
=========================

Divorce and Separation
----------------------
Divorce is the legal dissolution of a marriage by a court. Grounds for divorce vary by jurisdiction but commonly include:
- Irretrievable breakdown of the marriage
- Adultery
- Unreasonable behaviour
- Desertion (typically 2 years)
- Mutual consent after separation (1–2 years depending on jurisdiction)

Child Custody
-------------
Courts determine custody based on the "best interests of the child." Types include:
- Sole custody: one parent has primary physical and legal custody
- Joint custody: both parents share decision-making and/or residence
- Visitation rights: non-custodial parent gets scheduled time with child

Child Support
-------------
Non-custodial parents generally must pay financial support calculated based on income, number of children, and custody arrangement. Failure to pay can lead to wage garnishment or contempt proceedings.

Domestic Violence
-----------------
Victims can obtain restraining orders or protection orders. These legally prohibit the abuser from contacting or approaching the victim. Violations are criminal offences.

Alimony / Spousal Support
--------------------------
Courts may order one spouse to pay ongoing financial support to the other after divorce. Factors include length of marriage, standard of living, earning capacity, and contributions made.

Adoption
--------
Adoption legally creates a parent-child relationship. Requires court approval. Biological parental rights must be terminated first (voluntarily or by court order).
