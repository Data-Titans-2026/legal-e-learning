CIVIL RIGHTS — Key Concepts
============================
Protected Classes
-----------------
Race, colour, religion, sex, national origin, disability, age (40+), genetic information. Protected under the U.S. Constitution and statutes including the Civil Rights Act of 1964, ADA, and ADEA.

Discrimination in Housing
--------------------------
The Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in sale, rental, and financing based on race, colour, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, or disability. File complaints with HUD within 1 year.

Discrimination in Employment
-----------------------------
Title VII prohibits employment discrimination. File with EEOC within 180–300 days of the discriminatory act before suing. EEOC may investigate, mediate, or issue a "right to sue" letter.

Police Misconduct
-----------------
Excessive force, unlawful arrest, and civil rights violations can be challenged under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 (federal civil rights statute). File complaints with Internal Affairs, the civilian oversight board, or the DOJ Civil Rights Division.

ADA Accommodations
-------------------
Employers with 15+ employees must provide reasonable accommodations for qualified individuals with disabilities unless it causes undue hardship. Public places must also be accessible.

Voting Rights
-------------
The Voting Rights Act prohibits discriminatory voting practices. Voter suppression, intimidation, or illegal ID requirements can be reported to the DOJ or state election authorities.

Free Speech & Assembly
----------------------
The First Amendment protects speech, peaceful assembly, and petition against government (not private) suppression. Permits may be required for large public gatherings but cannot be denied based on viewpoint.
